How I work

Audit. Build. Automate. Document.

A tight four-step process that gets your system running quickly and keeps it running reliably.

STEP 1 OF 4

Discovery and audit

I map what is manual, broken or missing. For existing GHL accounts that means a full audit of workflow logic, pipeline structure, integration health and contact data.

STEP 2 OF 4

System design

The full architecture is drawn before anything is built: triggers, branching logic, integration touchpoints and failsafes. You see the blueprint first.

STEP 3 OF 4

Build and test

Workflows and integrations are built in staging and tested against every trigger, error state and edge case before they touch live contacts.

STEP 4 OF 4

Deploy and document

After launch I monitor for 48 to 72 hours, then hand over SOPs and a knowledge base so your team can run the system without me.
